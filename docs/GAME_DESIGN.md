# Egg Rivals — Current Design Direction

Status: approved design direction layered on the verified Moonwood 0.3.2-r2 engineering baseline.

## Product identity

Egg Rivals combines speed progression, defended egg theft, elemental hatching, collectible pets, personal bases, social trading, open-world interference, and optional first-to-five duels.

The game should remain easy to understand immediately while allowing deep collection, customization, mastery, and social expression.

## Rarity ladder

Common -> Uncommon -> Rare -> Epic -> Legendary -> Mythic -> Godly.

Godly is the highest standard rarity currently approved.

## Elemental incubators

Every base has four incubators: Fire, Water, Wind, and Earth.

The egg determines the creature. The incubator determines the elemental variant.

Incubators should be separated physically. Preferred square-base layout places one incubator at each corner to reduce accidental placement.

Each element needs unmistakable visual language: Fire, Water, Wind, and Earth effects, architecture, color, and animation.

Egg placement should use deliberate confirmation and a clear result preview rather than an accidental walk-over action.
## Pet pen

The free starter pen must already feel complete and enjoyable. It is not a deliberately weak feature that requires payment or upgrades to become usable.

Starter capabilities include visible resident pets, one active follower, passive income from owned pets, a walk-through gate, inspection, Favorite/Lock protection, and safe pet management.

Penned pets should exhibit autonomous social behavior such as wandering, sleeping, gathering, playing, and reacting to the owner.

When a new pet enters a pen area, resident pets hold a welcoming party before returning to normal behavior.

Godly pets create a special social hierarchy. Other pets keep their distance and do not casually play with a Godly pet.

Periodically, a Godly pet rises into the air and same-element pets stop their activity, orient toward it, and bow. Visitors may also trigger or witness this reverence event.

Pen progression branches through physical expansion, elemental habitats, creature comforts, showcase positions, management tools, visitor features, celebration upgrades, themes, and large-creature facilities.

Do not turn the pen into a feeding, health, breeding, or chore simulator unless that decision is made explicitly later.

## Treadmill and Speed Lab

The treadmill is a signature progression machine, not a passive number pad.

Approved directions include Momentum training, visible machine modules, charged Overdrive, movement mastery, timed Training Trials, personal ghost racing, elemental tuning, pet spectators, milestone celebrations, machine grades, heat/cooling presentation, social drafting, sprint challenges, and Motion Energy that can power camp presentation.

Large progression values must map to controllable Roblox movement through a bounded or diminishing-return movement function.
## Night

Night completely transforms presentation into a dark neon-blacklight world.

Normal daytime egg taking stops. One special high-value Night egg spawns at a randomly selected hidden location and must be discovered through exploration and subtle environmental clues.

Night presentation uses neon edges, glowing flora and signs, elemental color identities, particle trails on fast glowing objects, and impact bursts on genuine full-stop collisions.

Keep Night mechanically focused. Its power should come primarily from atmosphere, search tension, visual transformation, the hidden egg, and existing hatch acceleration rather than many unrelated systems.

## Respawn and homecoming

Each player base has a clearly designated personal respawn platform.

Respawn should feel like returning home: a brief visual arrival effect, readable safe placement, and pet attention or greeting where appropriate.

## Hub economy and shops

Approved locations include Trail Supplies, Ranch & Pen Works, Trading Post, Night Market, Trainer Workshop, Duel Armory, Pet Outfitter, Exchange, Quest Board / Ranger Station, and Elemental Bazaar.

The Exchange supports items, eggs, and pets. It is not limited to duplicates.

There is no Egg Appraiser.

## Design discipline

New features should reinforce the core loop rather than create disconnected minigames.

The verified 0.3.2-r2 source remains the engineering reference until a newer candidate passes equivalent regression.

Exact particle counts, visual thresholds, economy prices, huge-number balance, and final upgrade costs are tuning parameters unless explicitly locked later.
