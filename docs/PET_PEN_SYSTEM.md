# Egg Rivals — Pet Pen and Ranch System

Status: approved design direction.

The pet pen is not a storage box. It is the player's living collection, social showcase, and one of the main visual records of progression.

The starter pen must already feel complete and enjoyable. Upgrades add expression, organization, social value, and spectacle instead of repairing an intentionally weak starting experience.

## Free starter pen

Every player receives, at no upgrade cost:

- a proper fenced pen behind / integrated with the base;
- a walk-through gate;
- visible resident pets;
- one active companion that can follow the player;
- passive income from all owned pets, including penned pets;
- basic wandering, sleeping, sitting, gathering, and play behaviors;
- pet inspection and readable nameplates;
- Favorite / Lock protection;
- safe pet management;
- a visible earnings summary;
- a basic welcoming-party behavior for newly added pets.

The Collection remains authoritative for ownership. Physical display capacity never limits how many pets the player may own.
## Pet interaction menu

Selecting a pet in the pen should expose clear actions such as:

- Follow Me / Set Active;
- Send to Pen;
- Favorite / Lock;
- Trade;
- Exchange;
- Select as Duel Stake.

Actions that can change ownership or risk an item require explicit confirmation.

A locked/favorite pet cannot accidentally enter a trade, Exchange transaction, or duel stake.

## Nameplates and inspection

When a player is close enough, a pet may show concise information such as:

`FIRE DRAGON • EPIC • +12 coins / 2s`

Inspection can expose creature, element, rarity, income, cosmetic variant, and other collection metadata without cluttering the world at long distance.

Visitors may inspect pets but cannot move, stake, exchange, or otherwise mutate another player's collection.
## One active companion

Exactly one pet may be the active follower at a time.

The active pet follows behind or accompanies its owner without occupying the camera or obstructing movement. It can be changed through the Collection or physical pet interaction.

All other displayed pets remain at the ranch.

If the player has no active pet, the first newly hatched pet may become active automatically. A pet transferred through a duel defaults to the winner's pen unless explicitly activated later.

## Welcome party

A welcoming party is a **free baseline behavior**, not an upgrade.

When a new pet enters a ranch area for the first time:

1. nearby resident pets notice the arrival;
2. they move toward the entrance / arrival area;
3. they gather around the newcomer;
4. they perform a short cheer, bounce, or species-appropriate greeting;
5. they disperse and return to normal behavior.

The event should be charming and readable without becoming long enough to interrupt gameplay.
## Godly social hierarchy

Godly pets have a unique social presence.

Normal pets do not casually play with a Godly pet and maintain a respectful distance.

Periodically, a Godly pet performs a reverence event:

1. the Godly pet rises into the air;
2. pets of the same element stop their current activity;
3. they orient toward the Godly pet;
4. they bow;
5. the Godly pet descends;
6. normal behavior resumes.

Visitors may witness or trigger the reverence sequence.

At Night, the same event may receive stronger presentation such as a neon crown ring, aura, or intensified elemental lighting, while preserving the same underlying behavior.

This should feel rare and prestigious rather than spammed.
## Homecoming behavior

The ranch should notice its owner.

When the player returns to the base after being away, some pets may approach the fence or gate, look toward the player, or briefly greet them.

After death/respawn, the active companion and nearby penned pets may visually acknowledge the player's return.

These reactions reinforce attachment without creating feeding, happiness, or maintenance meters.

## Elemental habitat behavior

Elemental habitats are approved progression and presentation features.

Examples:

- Fire pets gather near a fire pit, ember stones, or volcanic rock.
- Water pets gather near a pond, fountain, or shallow pool.
- Wind pets prefer streamers, windmills, raised platforms, or airy perches.
- Earth pets prefer rocks, plants, logs, roots, and burrow-like spaces.

Initially these are behavioral and cosmetic identities, not mandatory stat bonuses.
## Progression branches

Pen progression is **not** a single elementary size/speed ladder. The approved branches are:

### Ranch Expansion
Adds meaningful physical sections such as side paddocks, rear paddocks, or large-animal areas instead of merely scaling one rectangle.

### Habitat Builder
Adds elemental habitat spaces and interactive environmental objects.

### Creature Comforts
Adds beds, shade shelters, toys, scratching posts, climbing objects, pools, caves, trees, perches, and resting platforms.

### Showcase Upgrades
Adds prominent stalls, podiums, rarity lighting, elemental backdrops, custom plaques, and Featured Pet positions.

### Pen Manager
Adds sorting by creature, element, rarity, favorites, saved groups, named areas, filters, and automatic display assignment.
### Visitor Features
Lets visitors inspect residents, initiate safe trade requests, and use harmless social reactions. Ownership authority never leaves the owner/server.

### Celebration Upgrades
Enhances the free welcome party with confetti, streamers, music, elemental effects, welcome arches, fireworks, and rarity-specific celebrations. Mythic and Godly arrivals should feel substantially more dramatic than Common arrivals.

### Pen Themes
Changes the ranch's overall aesthetic. Candidate themes include Forest Ranch, Castle Courtyard, Futuristic Habitat, Pirate Pen, Candy Ranch, Volcanic Sanctuary, and Arctic Preserve.

### Large Creature Facilities
Provides stalls, caves, pools, perches, or giant paddocks for oversized creatures so large pets feel intentionally housed rather than arbitrarily scaled down.

### Pet Activity Upgrades
Adds richer autonomous play: chasing, group naps, elemental interactions, watching the treadmill, sitting by the gate, cooperative play, and other species-appropriate behavior.

## Display capacity

Physical display capacity may grow through Ranch Expansion, but hidden overflow pets remain safely owned and continue to exist in the Collection.

The pen system may page / rotate displayed residents for performance. It must never destroy, sell, or lose a pet because the physical pen is full.
## Earnings board

The ranch should expose a simple world-space summary, for example:

`12 PETS • +87 COINS / 2s`

This makes the economic value of a collection visible without forcing the player into menus.

## Customization

Approved customizable presentation includes:

- fence material and style;
- gate style;
- ground treatment;
- signage;
- lights;
- elemental decorations;
- habitat objects;
- theme packages;
- showcase presentation.

Ranch & Pen Works is the natural in-world location for these systems.

## Explicitly excluded for now

Do **not** turn the pen into a second maintenance game.

The following are out of scope unless later approved:

- feeding meters;
- pet health chores;
- breeding systems;
- mandatory happiness maintenance;
- decay caused by not visiting the pen.

The pen exists to support collection, identity, social value, delight, and the primary Egg Rivals loop.
